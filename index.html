Who I Am

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies (MRIIRS), Faridabad, specialising in Data Science. My roots trace back to Faridabad, where I completed my +2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics — a foundation that still shapes how I approach problems: rigorously, and from first principles.

These days I spend most of my time at the intersection of software engineering and quantitative finance — writing C and Python for systems-level thinking, running numerical models in MATLAB, and building web interfaces that make market data legible. I'm working toward a CFA charter, and long-term I want to build tools that make financial analysis faster and more intuitive.

Outside of code and markets, I'm a bit of a film buff with a soft spot for direction and visual storytelling — usually with good music playing in the background.
